\subsection{Aliasing and diagnostic limits}

For positive integer K, let $h(\alpha)=(2/5)\sin^2(\pi K\alpha)$ and
\begin{equation}
 g(x_1,x_2)=\frac25x_1+\frac35x_2+(x_1-x_2)h((x_1+x_2)/2).
\label{eq:alias}
\end{equation}
With baseline (0,0), input (1,1) and reference 1, Q's path gradient is $(1-\alpha)(2/5+h,3/5-h)$. Every uniform budget m dividing K samples h=0. Trapezoidal and endpoint-inclusive estimates then give (1/5,3/10), with exact completeness and identical rankings. Symmetry gives $\int_0^1(1-\alpha)h(\alpha)\,d\alpha=1/10$, so exact IG is (3/10,1/5), reversing the order. Any finite set of uniform budgets can be hidden by a common multiple K. Completeness cancellation and grid agreement therefore cannot certify coordinates without additional regularity information. At K=128 the inaccurate order even has better insertion and deletion scores on the three-point intervention grid. Exact integration and perturbation-score optimization remain distinct. Full constructions, finite-stabilizer bounds and calibration derivations are in the supplementary derivations.

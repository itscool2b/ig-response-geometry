The [exact-edits ledger](exact_edits_checklist.md) records current action status; the [Final-v5 ledger](final_v5_checklist.md) preserves earlier dispositions and author boundaries. Headline PickCube records and the separate numerical collection have different preservation limits. Original observations, noise and checkpoint binaries are unavailable for headline replay; numerical contexts retained noise and cached encoder outputs for their offline function. The availability statement and Table 1 preserve this distinction. The five-step DPM-Solver++ statement describes the implementation configuration used for saved PickCube runs and numerical collection; the historical saved runs lack authenticated full sampler configuration. The Q-ranked episode records explicitly store five solver steps for all 750 retained calls; the N-ranked records omit solver steps and retain the default-configuration inference.

Funding, contributions, competing interests and mentor approval are user-confirmed author facts, outside the scientific evidence map. The author-deferred AI statement, independent human cold read and journal answers remain separate. Editor inquiries are unsent drafts. Public release authorization does not itself establish completed publication or journal submission; verified release state belongs to the publication receipts.

RMS is positive raw root-mean-square active-action discrepancy. For B > 0, endpoint normalization cancels its positive scale and gives the same curve as N with epsilon zero; B = 0 remains undefined. Positive epsilon smooths the scalar norm, while continuity of the full input gradient also requires a smooth action map.

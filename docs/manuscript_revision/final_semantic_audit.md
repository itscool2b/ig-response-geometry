# Final-v5 scientific meaning and review scope

Final-v5 preserves the current study, saved populations, finite effects, undefined cases and adverse results. It changes exposition and typesetting without new model runs, excluded-cohort rescoring, trimming or exclusions. Reporting version `2026-10-03-v2`, its source evidence and the original endpoint-roundoff policy remain unchanged. The independent Final-v5 scientific review checked all 14 paper items against 18 source/evidence files and 155 scientific input hashes without open findings. Earlier reviews authenticate only their earlier source bytes.

For fixed rankings and fully in-range curves, scoring with N instead of Q cannot increase normalized AUC. Strict decrease requires positive AUC weight on a node with 0 < r < B. The reverse response change has the reverse direction. Observed positive counts remain separate empirical facts. Threshold examples concern absolute cutoffs; the additive random-order example is not a measured RDT baseline. Appendix A.4 supplies a separate analytic reordering example.

The cohorts share recorded seed/episode/call identifiers and seven extreme vision-insertion episodes, but all 750 aligned baseline residuals differ. Vision log-B correlation is 0.992 at call 0, 0.980 at call 1 and 0.289 at call 2, then varies between about 0.4 and 0.8, ending at 0.658. This is not a steady decline. Unavailable historical contexts prevent fully matched ranking comparisons or claims of independent replication. The short four-task verification set is distinct from the numerical collection containing call 12. Cross-seed RDT-1B comparisons retain vision only because language baselines differ.

First-call and later-call contributions use all 750 calls; conditional later-call averages use 720. All original means, medians, episode intervals and omission diagnostics remain available. The three zero-spanning mean intervals are Q-ranked language deletion [-0.102, 0.063], N-ranked language insertion [-0.096, 0.027] and N-ranked language deletion [-4.208, 0.078]. They are distinct from Table 2's median intervals.

The numerical budget ladder is 128/256/512 intervals for vision and language and 256/512/1,024 for state. The 11,592 total checks comprise 6,048 budget-sensitive checks (302 violate, 5,602 satisfy and 144 are not applicable) and 5,544 other checks, all satisfied. Thus 11,146 satisfy overall. The 144 not-applicable checks are top-five-percent overlap comparisons with zero realized selected groups. Budget-sensitive checks include dependent criteria across IG and mean path-gradient diagnostics and conditional completeness checks at coarse budgets; they are not counts of independent contexts. The 5,544 other checks include 2,880 repeatability and coverage checks, of which 1,800 are equality checks. These counts are recomputed from the sealed numerical projection's criterion names and statuses, rather than treating every non-repeat check as a budget check.

Vision coordinate changes of 2.05% and 1.23% compare 256 with 512 intervals. Completeness residuals of 0.124% and 0.045% belong to the 512 maps; the matching 256-map residuals are 2.17% and 1.25%. Passing finest-budget completeness does not prove coordinate convergence, but this example does not show completeness missing the 256-map change. The two PickSingleYCB episode-1 state contexts have Q residuals above 100% specifically at 256 intervals. Appendix B.1 supplies the stronger example: wrong coarse coordinates and order despite exact completeness cancellation. Its endpoint-inclusive coarse identity establishes the coarse failure; the 2K-grid expression instead has the correct order with inexact values.

Sampler provenance must distinguish implementation configuration from the unavailable historical run configuration. The target-reversal proof retains the positive finite-epsilon multiplier and its assumptions. The aliasing proof keeps exact values distinct from coarse results. These clarifications do not establish an optimal integration budget, ranking efficacy or robot task improvement.

The publication clarification records the saved coordinate, rank and curve thresholds, their near-zero conditions, and the absence of a selected budget. State has one active coordinate per feature group; saved PickCube rescoring covers vision and language, while numerical diagnostics include state. The extreme normalized response combines a small baseline residual with large intermediate residuals. Figure 5 names equality checks precisely. These edits preserve the sealed result statuses and all scientific inputs.

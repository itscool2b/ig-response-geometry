# Corrections to the audit

These corrections preserve the audit's intended revision while checking its assertions against the actual source evidence.

The [Final-v5 ledger](final_v5_checklist.md) controls current action status; the Round 3 ledger and entries below retain their historical context. The evidence interpretations below explain the corrected manuscript; source, display and package acceptance is bound separately to the final delivery manifest and verification receipts.

1. Across all eight cases, calls with at least one saved overshoot range from 16.0% to 74.0%. Insertion cases range from 38.9% to 74.0%. The audit's shorter 42% to 74% description is not the all-case range.
2. A deleted language position receives the matching adapted baseline representation. Zero-padded encoder outputs pass through the learned adaptor, so the substituted vector need not be zero. The contextual encoder is not rerun.
3. At the actual input, the quadratic target is zero but the stabilized negative norm is minus the square root of epsilon. Their gradients vanish under the stated differentiability assumptions.
4. Saved Q endpoints imply zero residual. Native L2 endpoints are floating-point representations of minus one millionth and use the existing documented domain-roundoff clamp. The theorem's exact arithmetic assumptions must not be confused with saved-float equality.
5. The approximately -4438.8 example is the extreme Q-ranked vision-insertion call. A norm-ranked vision-insertion call has a more negative change, approximately -5242.9.
6. The numerical audit's 302 violations count dependent criteria. They do not count independent contexts or exclusively IG failures. The new display distinguishes IG from path-gradient checks.
7. Hama and colleagues' individual straight-line-referenced criterion has zero random-order expectation in the additive case. Their symmetric insertion/deletion combination has the stronger interaction-robust zero expectation. These claims are distinct.
8. Exact recovery at twice the aliasing frequency belongs to the stated trapezoid construction. It must not be transferred unchanged to the historical equal-endpoint-weight rule.
9. Rounded in-range and overshoot contributions need not add exactly to a separately rounded mean. Verify the decomposition at full precision and describe displayed values as rounded.
10. The diagnostic snapshot contains twelve completed contexts and predates the eventual compute stop. It is not a verified final census at that stop.

The complete twelve-context maps and common-response curves are unavailable in the inspected local public and private workspaces. The optional matched-ranking analysis is therefore omitted. Earlier reports with different numerical functions and the selected two-context state subset are not substituted.

The final scientific review also corrected nominal versus realized intervention fractions. Saved AUC grids record nominal percentages. Integer token counts can change realized fractions, and the complete historical masks are unavailable. The manuscript retains the recorded nominal grid for within-curve rescoring and does not claim recovery of actual token counts.

Their related initial states and shared extreme episodes must be described without treating the cohorts as independent evidence or claiming a causal explanation for similar medians.

## Round 3 corrections and limits

1. Nonnegative in-range response differences are guaranteed by the identity. A strict positive AUC difference additionally requires at least one point with positive AUC weight and residual strictly between zero and B. Being an interior schedule node alone is insufficient. Fully in-range prevalence and positivity among overshooting calls are measured facts, not consequences of algebra alone.
2. The absolute 0.40 bar demonstrates threshold dependence. The additive response-matched references put the motivating deletion median about 0.21 below its reference under either response. They are analytic calibration, not measured RDT random-ranking controls. A nonconstant response shift permits ranking-order changes; its observed spread across calls does not itself demonstrate a matched real-model ranking reversal.
3. Shared seed/episode labels and the same seven extreme episodes restrict independence claims. All-baseline residuals differ even at corresponding first calls; strongly related does not mean nearly identical. A seed-42 first-call pair differs by roughly a factor of 13. Missing full observations, noise and original checkpoint binaries still prevent replay of headline rankings.
4. Noise availability differs by collection. The separate numerical collection, capped at 13 calls per episode, preserved noise and cached encoder outputs. The saved headline PickCube curves did not preserve their original noise arrays. Numerical checks apply to the offline fp32 function and do not retrospectively validate m=64 bf16 saved rankings.
5. The existing conversion corrects tiny storage-roundoff residuals at the actual-input endpoint, whose exact residual is zero by definition. The absence of intervention undershoots and the absence of AUC/difference clipping are different claims. Do not describe this endpoint correction as trimming observed effects.
6. A single ranking target does not prevent rescoring fixed curves. Excluding other sets is a current-study and checked-processing scope decision, not a mathematical requirement for two targets. The fine-tuned 1B records have 15 successes among 60 episodes and provide a direct possible extension. The author chose not to add an excluded-cohort robustness table in this revision.
7. The numerical sample has 24 planned contexts: 12 complete, nine unfinished and three whose selected call was not reached. Neither later completion nor an independent population failure rate is inferred. The 302 budget violations count dependent criteria; the 2,880 repeat/coverage passes include 1,800 equality checks. Finest-pair failure counts must name whether either IG or path-gradient criterion can flag the context arm.
8. Numerical changes can affect evaluation or leave it unchanged. PickSingleYCB episode 1 call 0 state Q has about 0.83 rank agreement and a 0.19 insertion-AUC change from 256 to 512 intervals. Call 12 state Q has a 32.1% coordinate change with unchanged recorded curves. The latter does not justify claiming numerical error is always invisible to evaluation.
9. First-call contributions use the full 750-call mean denominator. Later-call contributions use that same denominator, while later-only averages divide by 720. For N-ranked vision insertion the later contribution is about -0.81 and the later-only average about -0.84. The first-call median is -0.845. These quantities must not be added or named interchangeably.
10. Dropping the nominal language 1% node is a saved-schedule sensitivity calculation, not recovery of realized masks. Median movement is about 0.004 at most; the in-range per-call bound is about 0.005. Both responses retain the same weights in each comparison. Token counts from the separate numerical collection do not establish counts in headline runs.
11. Endpoint-inclusive average and trapezoid estimators need distinct accuracy explanations. Their relation establishes the former's first-order term; the trapezoid citation does not directly establish it. At positive epsilon, approximate halving of sampled N residuals does not imply a gradient discontinuity or an asymptotic convergence theorem. Linear integrands are integrated exactly by both uniform rules.
12. Hama's IG ordering argument assumes a linear inner score and constant gradient direction. The interaction-robust zero-random-expectation statement concerns the symmetric combination on its full prefix grid. Do not transfer it to the sparse nine-point schedule. Appendix A.2 supplies a separate exact-IG construction; its claims are analytic, not a matched RDT comparison.
13. IDGI retains the component of each integration step along the gradient. Model-randomization discussion needs the Adebayo citation. Citation order and metadata must follow the cited sources, including the RDT release; do not force an assumed volume, year or reference count. The Online exporter checks its count against the compiled bibliography.
14. Analytic constructions become Figure 4 and numerical diagnostics Figure 5. Change panels use finer budget 2m; completeness uses m. Nominal AUC schedule nodes and IG integration nodes are separate concepts. Symlog thresholds belong in the relevant captions.
15. Typical observation marks use a deterministic median-nearest call from all 750 Q-ranked vision-insertion calls, with a record-ID tie break. They illustrate scale without filtering the numerical population or replacing the extreme example.
16. Technical readiness does not independently verify author declarations or replace human review. The author has supplied funding, contributions and competing-interest declarations and confirmed mentor approval; the author retains responsibility for these facts and the AI statement. Editor inquiries and prior-version notes remain unsent drafts. An independent human cold read remains separate.

These are dependent criteria, not independent contexts or exclusively IG failures; subtracting repeatability/coverage alone does not identify the budget-sensitive subset.

5. Five-step DPM-Solver++ is implementation configuration relevant to saved PickCube runs and the numerical collection, not authenticated historical run-configuration evidence. Preserve the qualification that the historical complete sampler configuration was not saved.
6. The endpoint-inclusive coarse-grid identity in Appendix B.1 establishes the coarse ordering failure. Its 2K-grid equation has the correct order but inexact values. An exact-attribution label must not call these exact values aliased.

7.
